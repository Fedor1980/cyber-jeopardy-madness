# NexusOS Brand Guidelines

## Logo Usage

### Primary Logo
Use the full-color logo on white backgrounds.

### Logo Variations
- Full color: nexusos_logo_full_color.svg
- White: nexusos_logo_white.svg (on dark backgrounds)
- Black: nexusos_logo_black.svg (on light backgrounds)
- Icon only: nexusos_icon_only.svg

### Clear Space
Maintain clear space around logo equal to the height of "OS" in logo.

### Minimum Size
- Digital: 120px wide
- Print: 1 inch wide

## Voice and Tone
- Professional yet approachable
- Technical but accessible
- Confident without being arrogant
- Helpful and supportive
